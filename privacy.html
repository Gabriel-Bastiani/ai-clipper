IA Clipper Privacy Policy

IA Clipper accesses only the owner's own TikTok account, to upload videos and read basic profile and video statistics.

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared or sold.

No data from other TikTok users is collected.

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings.
